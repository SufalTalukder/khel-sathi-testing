Uttar Pradesh State Rifle Association

About the Association

Uttar Pradesh State Rifle Association (UPSRA) is located in Lucknow, U. P. Established in 1964, ours is one of the oldest State Rifle Associations in India. UPSRA organize Pre-State Level and State Level shooting competitions in view of welfare of UP Shooters and promotion of Shooting Sports. Date of establishment: 22.08.1964
Affiliations:
UPSRA is affiliated with:
o U.P. Olympic Association
o The National Rifle Association of India
o Registrar of Societies, Firms and Chits
Competitions organized by UPSRA:
o UPSRA has organized 47 state level competitions. This year it will be 48th edition.
o UPSRA has successfully organized 24 States Inter School Level Competitions.
o UPSRA has organized 25 Pre-State level competitions.
All India Level Competitions:
UPSRA has also organized various all India level shooting competitions:
o All India G.V. Mavlankar shooting competition
o All India Digvijay Singh memorial shooting competition
o All India Kumar Surendra Singh memorial inter school shooting competition
