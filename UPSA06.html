Uttar Pradesh Swimming Associaton

About the Association

The UPSA had been Registered under Firms & Societies Act of U.P. Government and was formed in the year1950. Since then, regular activities and several competitions of State, & National Championships had been organised under the UPSA banner. Elections & AGM of UPSA had been regularly performed as per Government Guidelines and as per Sports Code of U.P. Samanvaye Championships of UPSA & U.P. Government have regularly been organised by UPSA without any year Gap.
